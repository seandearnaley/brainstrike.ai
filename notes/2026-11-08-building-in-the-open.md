As of today, Brainstrike’s source is public under the MIT license: [github.com/seandearnaley/brainstrike](https://github.com/seandearnaley/brainstrike).

Brainstrike is a desktop app for macOS, Windows and Linux. It runs agents inside your folders and shows every file they read, every search they run and every line they change.

From here on I’m building it in the open. These notes are where I’ll write down what changed, what broke and what’s next, one release at a time.

## Follow along

- Read the code, or open an issue when something breaks: [the repository](https://github.com/seandearnaley/brainstrike)
- Get each new note in your reader: [the feed](/notes/feed.xml)
- Download the newest beta: [brainstrike.ai](/)
