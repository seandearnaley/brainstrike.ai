Welcome to the Brainstrike dev notes.

Brainstrike is a desktop app for macOS, Windows and Linux. It runs agents inside your folders and shows every file they read, every search they run and every line they change.

I’m building it as a solo developer, and these notes are where I’ll write down what changed, what broke and what’s next, one release at a time.

- Read the code, or open an issue when something breaks: [the repository](https://github.com/seandearnaley/brainstrike)
